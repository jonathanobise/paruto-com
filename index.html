    How we build

    One standard, every product.

    Every Paruto company and product is held to the same bar, from the first page you see to the last setting you change.

    • Designed to Apple's guidelines

      Clear hierarchy, 44-point touch targets, real dark mode and controls that behave the way you expect.

    • Fast on any phone

      Pages are built to a performance budget. Rich 3D and motion load after the page is ready, never in your way.

    • Private by default

      Cookieless analytics and no advertising trackers. We collect only what a feature needs to work.

    • Light, dark and yours

      Every product follows your system appearance, and lets you choose light, dark and how dark it gets.

    • Accessible to everyone

      Screen readers, keyboards, larger text, Reduce Motion and Increase Contrast are designed in, not bolted on.

    • Crafted in 3D

      WebGL and motion are used where they add delight: a hero you fly through, worlds you explore, moments worth celebrating.

    The mark & its meaning

    P for Paruto.
    D for Dominance.

    Drawn as one continuous line. The P is the name; the D is the declaration — a commitment to lead in every space Paruto enters. Neither letter can be lifted out without breaking the other.

    1. P

      Paruto

      The name — the people, the promise. Every engagement begins and ends with the Paruto standard.

    2. D

      Dominance

      The ambition — to lead and define every space we enter, in Africa and beyond.

    3. ↗

      Direction

      The forward curve — a speech bubble and an arrow. Momentum, delivered outward.
