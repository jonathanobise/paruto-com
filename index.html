    The mark & its meaning

    P for Paruto.
    D for Dominance.

    Drawn as one continuous line. The P is the name; the D is the declaration — a commitment to lead in every space Paruto enters. Neither letter can be lifted out without breaking the other.

    1. P

      Paruto

      The name — the people, the promise. Every engagement begins and ends with the Paruto standard.

    2. D

      Dominance

      The ambition — to lead and define every space we enter, in Africa and beyond.

    3. ↗

      Direction

      The forward curve — a speech bubble and an arrow. Momentum, delivered outward.
