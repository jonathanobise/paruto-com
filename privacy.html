Privacy

What this site collects.

Very little. paruto.com has no accounts, no forms, no cookies and no advertising, and it doesn't sell or share data.

Visit statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits: which pages are viewed, the site that referred you, your browser and device type, your country, and how quickly pages load. Cloudflare states that this service doesn't use cookies or local storage and doesn't fingerprint individual visitors. We only see totals, never who you are. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Your appearance choice

If you choose Light or Dark in the menu or footer, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. Choosing Auto removes it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver pages and keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts and scripts are served from paruto.com itself, not from third parties.

When you email us

If you write to hello@paruto.com, we receive your email address and whatever you send, and use it only to reply. Our email is hosted by Zoho Mail.

Other sites

Links to Paruto companies, our apps and our social media profiles lead to sites with their own privacy policies.

Questions or requests

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email hello@paruto.com.
